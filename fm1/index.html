Six channels

Six equal channels. Any sound on any of them.

No fixed drum track. Any channel can be a synth, a drum kit, a sampler or a MIDI output, and each one plays up to six notes at once.

ch 1BASS
ch 2ARP
ch 3LEAD
ch 4KEYS
ch 5PAD
ch 6DRUMS

A song, named your way: SHIFT + SAVE renames any channel.

Made for playing

Everything within reach of two hands.

PLAY is home

The sound wave of what you're playing, with the chord name under it. SHIFT + HOME switches to LOOP and back.

Four knobs, every screen

The four knobs always change what you see on the screen. No menus to dig through mid-song.

Fourteen engines

Pick a channel, pick a sound. From analog, FM and supersaw stacks to granular, physical models, samples and drums.

Press REC to record

Stopped, REC waits for your first note; playing, it records at once. While you play or record, the screen shows LOOP: four channels and their steps, each pattern one to eight bars long.

Punch-ins while you play

Hold FX and the keys become 27 punch-in effects, from loop repeats and filter sweeps to EQ kills, while the pattern keeps playing.

Scenes and songs

Put patterns together in scenes and launch them live with SAVE and a key. Chain scenes into songs, and keep eight songs on the FM-1. SHIFT + SAVE gives a channel its own name.

The screens

Clear on a small screen.

Real screens from the firmware, in both themes. PLAY is home; while you play or record, the screen shows LOOP, and SHIFT + HOME switches between them.

Why nullwave

A simpler way to play the FM-1.

nullwave fm1 builds on ideas from other FM-1 projects, with one goal: make the FM-1 simpler to play.
