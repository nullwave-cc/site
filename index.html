What we do

Small instruments, played well.

Lots of good music hardware is held back by its software. nullwave makes software that is simple to play, works with the instruments people already own, and helps them learn along the way.

01

Open source

Every nullwave product is free and open, so anyone can read it, change it or build on it.

02

Made to be played

Fewer menus and settings, more time playing. Every screen is built for two hands on an instrument.

03

One family

The products share one name and one design, and they're made to work together.

Products

One name, three products... so far.

nullwave fm1 comes first. nullwave learn is next, then nullwave create.

01

nullwave fm1

Firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a six-channel groovebox with fourteen sound engines and a sequencer for playing live.

In development →
02

nullwave learn

An app for learning music theory on keyboard and guitar: notes, scales, chords and reading music, learned by playing. More instruments may follow.

Coming soon
03

nullwave create

A sampler, looper and small DAW that works alongside nullwave fm1: record, loop and arrange what you play.

Coming soon
About

Made by one person, in the open.

Hi, I'm Adro Morelos.

nullwave started with something small. I wanted to make an app to help my wife learn songs on the keyboard, and pick up a little theory along the way. One idea led to another, and it grew into this.

AI is what makes it possible for one engineer to build all of this. But built with AI doesn't mean thrown together: every part is carefully crafted, and every nullwave product is made with care.
